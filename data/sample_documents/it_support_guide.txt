COMPANY IT SUPPORT AND TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE
============================================

1. PASSWORD RESET PROCEDURE

Employees who forget their company account password should visit
the organization's official password reset portal.

Employees must verify their identity using their registered email
or multi-factor authentication (MFA).

If the password reset process fails, employees should contact
the IT Helpdesk.

2. ACCOUNT LOCKOUT

A company account may become temporarily locked after five
consecutive incorrect login attempts.

Employees should wait 15 minutes before trying again or contact
the IT Helpdesk for assistance.

3. EMAIL CONFIGURATION

Employees must use their official company email account
for business communication.

Email access is available through the organization's approved
webmail portal or configured email application.

4. VPN ACCESS

Remote employees must connect through the organization's
approved Virtual Private Network (VPN).

Employees should use their assigned credentials and complete
MFA verification before accessing internal resources.

5. SOFTWARE INSTALLATION

Employees must obtain IT department approval before installing
new software on company-owned devices.

Only authorized and licensed software may be installed.

6. HARDWARE ISSUES

Employees experiencing laptop, keyboard, mouse or monitor problems
should raise a support ticket with the IT Helpdesk.

The ticket should include the device asset ID, problem description
and any relevant error messages.

7. IT HELPDESK SUPPORT

Employees can report technical issues through the company's
IT service management portal.

Each request receives a unique ticket number for tracking.

Employees should provide accurate details to help the IT team
resolve issues efficiently.
