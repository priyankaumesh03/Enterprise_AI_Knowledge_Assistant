COMPANY HR POLICY HANDBOOK
==========================

1. ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY

Employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Leave requests should be submitted to the reporting manager at least
three working days in advance.

Emergency leave may be requested with immediate notification to the manager.

2. SICK LEAVE POLICY

Employees are entitled to 12 days of sick leave per year.

Employees taking sick leave for more than two consecutive working days
may be required to submit a medical certificate.

3. WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Employees may work from home up to two days per week, subject to
manager approval and business requirements.

Employees must remain available during official working hours.

4. WORKING HOURS

The standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

Employees are expected to complete eight working hours per day,
excluding lunch breaks.

5. EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING

New employees must complete the onboarding process during their
first five working days.

The onboarding process includes document verification, IT account
creation, company policy orientation and team introduction.

6. REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Employees can claim eligible business travel and approved
work-related expenses.

Expense claims must include valid receipts and manager approval.

Claims should be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
