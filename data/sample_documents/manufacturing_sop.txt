MANUFACTURING OPERATIONS AND SAFETY HANDBOOK
============================================

1. MACHINE OPERATION PROCEDURE

Employees must receive proper training before operating
manufacturing equipment.

Operators must inspect the machine before starting production.

Any unusual noise, vibration or overheating must be reported
to the maintenance department immediately.

2. PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE

Preventive maintenance involves regularly inspecting and servicing
equipment to prevent unexpected failures.

Production machines should undergo scheduled maintenance
according to the manufacturer's recommendations.

Maintenance activities must be recorded in the equipment
maintenance log.

3. WORKPLACE SAFETY

Employees must wear appropriate personal protective equipment
(PPE), including safety shoes, gloves and protective eyewear.

Emergency exits must remain accessible at all times.

Employees must never operate equipment without proper training.

4. EQUIPMENT FAILURE

If equipment stops working unexpectedly, operators must stop
using the machine and inform the maintenance supervisor.

Employees must not attempt unauthorized repairs.

All equipment failures must be recorded in the maintenance
reporting system.

5. QUALITY CONTROL

Manufactured products must undergo quality inspections
before being approved for dispatch.

Products that fail quality checks must be separated
and reported to the quality control department.

6. EMERGENCY PROCEDURES

Employees must follow the company's emergency response plan
during fire, equipment accidents or other emergencies.

Emergency incidents must be reported to the safety supervisor.

Employees must follow instructions from authorized
emergency response personnel.
